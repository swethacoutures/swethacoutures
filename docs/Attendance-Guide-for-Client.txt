====================================================================
  SWETHA'S COUTURE — FINGERPRINT ATTENDANCE
  A short guide for the owner
====================================================================

Employees press their finger on the machine. Everything else —
hours, days, salary — is worked out by the app on its own.
There is no daily task for you.

This page covers the three things you actually need: adding a
person, how their hours are counted, and how to fix a mistake.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ADDING A NEW EMPLOYEE  —  the one rule
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The machine does not know anybody's name. All it ever sends us
is a NUMBER. So the number in the app and the number on the
machine MUST BE THE SAME.

    IN THE APP                       ON THE MACHINE
    Employees > S.No  =  5    <-->   User ID  =  5

Give each new person the next free number: 1, 2, 3 ...
NEVER reuse a number that once belonged to someone else — their
old attendance would join the new person's record.


STEP 1 — In the app:  Employees  >  Add Employee

    Full Name .................. how they appear everywhere
    Pay basis * ................ Monthly salary / Daily wage /
                                 Rate per hour
    Amount * ................... the box renames itself to match
                                 (e.g. Monthly salary 18000)
    Standard hours per day * ... a full day for this person,
                                 usually 8
    Fingerprint device number (S.No)
                                 THE NUMBER ABOVE. Type it now,
                                 even before they have ever
                                 touched the machine.

    Phone, email, role, department, bonus — all optional.

    Save. You will see a green line confirming:
    "Punches from device number 5 will count towards this employee."


STEP 2 — On the machine (stand there with the employee)

    Press  M/OK  >  User Mgt.  >  New User

    User ID ...... the SAME number you typed as S.No.
                   This is the field that matters. Get it wrong
                   and their hours go to nobody.
    Name ......... optional, the app can pull it in later
    Fingerprint .. place the finger 3 times as prompted.
                   Use an index finger, pressed flat.
    ESC .......... to save

    If the machine says the User ID is already taken, that number
    belongs to somebody else. Back out, pick a free number, and
    correct the S.No in the app to match.


STEP 3 — Check it worked (one punch proves everything)

    Ask them to press their finger once, then open Attendance:

    Punches ..... the press appears within about 30 seconds
    Employees ... they are listed with their real name and pay.
                  If an ORANGE BANNER says "device number not
                  connected", the two numbers do not match —
                  go back and fix the S.No.
    Records ..... today's row exists, showing a check-in

    Still showing as a bare number instead of a name?
    Attendance > Employees > "Get names from device".


--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW A DAY IS COUNTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------

A day is made of PERIODS. A period is one check-in and the
check-out that follows it. The day's work is all the periods
added together. The Records tab shows every period on the row.

    09:00 in                     PERIOD 1  =  4 h 00
    13:00 out  --+
                 |  lunch, 1 h — not paid
    14:00 in   --+
    18:00 out                    PERIOD 2  =  4 h 00
                                 -------------------
                                 DAY TOTAL =  8 h 00

THE APP DECIDES CHECK-IN / CHECK-OUT, NOT THE MACHINE.
The 1st press of the day is a check-in, the 2nd a check-out,
the 3rd a check-in again, and so on. The machine's own label is
ignored completely — it comes from whichever mode key the
terminal was left in and is often wrong.

A fuller day, to show how it adds up:

    09:00 in    arrived
    09:30 out   went out for a call     ) 30 min, NOT paid
    10:00 in    came back               )
    13:00 out   lunch                   ) 1 hour, NOT paid
    14:00 in    back from lunch         )
    17:00 out   short break             ) 5 min, STILL PAID
    17:05 in    back after 5 minutes    )
    18:00 out   went home

    1. 09:00 -> 09:30     30m
    2. 10:00 -> 13:00     3h
    3. 14:00 -> 18:00     4h   (the 5-min break merged in)
                          ---------------
    In the shop  9 h 00
    PAID         7 h 30


TWO RULES THAT KEEP THE MATHS HONEST
(both changeable in Attendance > Payroll > Working rules)

  * Presses within 5 MINUTES of each other count as ONE press.
    People jab the sensor three or four times when they are not
    sure it read. Without this rule, one arrival would become
    four tiny periods and the day would collapse to a few
    minutes.

  * An absence is only a break if it is 20 MINUTES OR MORE.
    Stepping outside for three minutes stays paid. Going for
    lunch does not.

  IF YOU ARE TESTING THIS YOURSELF: punching five times inside
  two minutes will NOT show separate periods — it is read as one
  arrival, which is correct. To see real periods, leave more
  than 5 minutes between presses and more than 20 minutes for
  the break in the middle.


THE TWO NUMBERS ON SCREEN — they are not the same thing

The Records tab shows both, side by side, on every row:

  "In the shop" ............ first punch of the day to the last.
                             Lunch is still inside it. Grey.

  "Paid hours" ............. the periods added up, with real
                             breaks removed. Green. THIS IS THE
                             FIGURE THAT BECOMES SALARY.

  Click the pencil on any row and you also see the periods the
  machine recorded, so you can see what a correction does to the
  pay before you save it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW THE HOURS BECOME MONEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  Monthly salary ... converted to an hourly rate and paid on the
                     hours actually worked:
                     rate = salary / (working days x standard hours)
                     Arriving late and staying on still earns a
                     full day. Leaving early docks only the hours
                     missed.

  Daily wage ....... a flat amount for every day they check in.

  Rate per hour .... rate x the hours actually worked.

  OVERTIME is counted A DAY AT A TIME, against that person's
  standard day. Work 9 hours on a 8-hour day and that is 1 hour
  of overtime — it is NOT cancelled out by a short day later in
  the month.

  When anyone has overtime, an orange message appears at the top
  of Payroll naming them, the hours, and what those hours are
  worth. The hours are ALREADY INCLUDED in the salary below, at
  their normal hourly rate — the message is there so paying it
  is your decision and not a surprise.

  To pay overtime differently, or not at all, open that day on
  Records and use "Set the paid hours myself".

  On a DAILY WAGE the day rate is flat, so overtime is shown but
  nothing extra is added. The message says so.

  Attendance > Payroll > pick the month > Mark paid.
  Export the whole month to Excel with "Export Excel".
  Paid the wrong person? Press Undo — it reverses and says so.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHEN SOMEONE FORGETS TO PUNCH  —  you have full control
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The app NEVER guesses a missing punch. When a check-out is
missing, the row shows the unfinished period as

    3. 17:05 -> ?

in orange, marks the day "No check-out", and pays only the
periods that DID close. The unfinished stretch pays nothing
until you fix it. That is deliberate — better than quietly
paying a wrong number.

Everything below is on  Attendance > Records:

  Forgot to check OUT ......... pencil on the row, type the
                               check-out time
  Forgot to check IN .......... pencil on the row, type the
                               check-in time
  Missing the whole day ....... "Add" — pick the person, the
                               date and the times
  A day that should not exist .. bin icon on the row
  The hours are simply wrong ... pencil > "Set the paid hours
                               myself" > type the hours. This
                               beats every rule, for a day you
                               agreed to pay differently. The
                               row then shows a "Paid 8 hrs"
                               badge so nobody wonders later.
  A stray duplicate press ..... Attendance > Punches > bin icon

A day you have corrected by hand is LOCKED. The machine can
never overwrite it again.

Every edit, deletion and payment is recorded under
Attendance > Activity — who did it, when, and what changed.
Nothing is ever silently rewritten.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  Orange banner: "device number not connected"
      The number on the machine matches no S.No in the app.
      Employees > open the person > fix the S.No.

  Employee shows as a bare number
      Nobody has named them. Attendance > Employees >
      "Get names from device", or type the name.

  "Needs setup" in Payroll
      No pay basis or amount set — they count as 0.
      Employees > open them > choose a basis and amount.

  No punches arriving at all
      The terminal cannot reach the internet. On the machine:
      M/OK > COMM. > Ethernet. Gateway and DNS must NOT be
      0.0.0.0. (This is the most common cause by far.)

  Device time looks wrong
      Press "Sync clock" on the bar at the top of Attendance.
      It also corrects itself automatically every 6 hours.

  Everyone shows Absent today
      It is a weekly off day (Sunday by default), or the device
      is offline — check the bar at the top of Attendance.

  A finger is not recognised
      Re-enrol on the machine under the SAME User ID, and add a
      second finger as a backup.
